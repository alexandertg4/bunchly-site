Good to know

Which iPhones work?

Any iPhone with iOS 26. To check, open Settings, then General, then Software Update.

Do I need Developer Mode or a special setting?

No. TestFlight is made by Apple and installs Bunchly the normal way. There's nothing to turn on.

What's different about a beta?

Not much. Updates arrive through TestFlight (it can update automatically), and each version works for 90 days, so keep TestFlight on your phone. When Bunchly reaches the App Store, you can switch over and keep everything.

How do I tell you something's wrong?

In Bunchly, go to Settings, then Send feedback. Or take a screenshot and share it to TestFlight, or write to hello@bunchly.app. A real person reads every one.

Is my family's information safe?

The beta is the real Bunchly, with the same privacy. Most things never leave your iPhone, and what your family shares is locked so we can't read it. Read the privacy policy.
