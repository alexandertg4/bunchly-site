Private by design

Locked before it leaves

Your family's notes, reminders and plans are encrypted on your phone. Our server only stores locked boxes it can't open.

AI that stays home

The morning brief and suggestions use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI.

Location stays put

Store reminders check where you are on your phone. Your location never reaches us.

Questions

What does it cost?

The beta is free. When Bunchly reaches the App Store, the core app stays free, with an optional Plus plan later.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no password. Sharing with your family works through one-time invite links.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Bunchly is built so Android phones can join the same family later.

Is it only for families?

Families come first, but any bunch works: a trip with friends, a team, a party you're planning.

How do I get help?

Visit support or write to hello@bunchly.app.
